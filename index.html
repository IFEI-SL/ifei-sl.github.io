WHO WE ARE

Inclusive Financial Empowerment Initiative – Sierra Leone

Inclusive Financial Empowerment Initiative (IFEI) is a Sierra Leone-based initiative focused on inclusive financial empowerment, entrepreneurship and sustainable livelihoods. IFEI gives particular attention to persons with disabilities, women, youth, low-income households, vulnerable communities and small-scale entrepreneurs. Our approach goes beyond short-term credit by combining financial literacy, savings, entrepreneurship, productive assets, mentorship, market linkages and follow-up support.

Our Commitment

We are committed to reducing barriers to economic participation and supporting people to develop the knowledge, resources and opportunities needed for sustainable livelihoods.


From economic exclusion to opportunity, dignity and sustainable livelihoods.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

A Sierra Leone where underserved people, particularly persons with disabilities, women and young entrepreneurs, have fair access to financial knowledge, economic opportunities, productive resources and sustainable livelihoods.

Our Mission

To expand inclusive financial and economic opportunities by combining financial literacy, savings, entrepreneurship, SME development, skills, appropriate financing, productive assets, mentorship and market connections, while working with communities and partners to reduce barriers to participation.

FINANCIAL EMPOWERMENT

Financial Products & Support Services

Our financial empowerment approach combines financial education, savings, appropriate financing pathways and business support.

Financial Literacy

Budgeting, money management, responsible borrowing and financial decision-making.

Savings Support

Encouraging realistic savings habits and improved financial planning.

Appropriate Financing Pathways

Exploring suitable financing options for viable income-generating activities, subject to assessment and available resources.

Business Development

Business planning, bookkeeping, pricing, marketing and enterprise coaching.

Productive Assets

Support pathways involving productive tools, equipment and assistive devices.

Mentorship & Market Connections

Mentorship, business guidance, communication and market linkages.

Important: Specific financing, grants, loans and other forms of support are subject to programme development, beneficiary assessment, eligibility, partnerships and available resources. This website does not promise automatic financing.
OUR APPROACH

The Beneficiary Pathway

Our intended model connects people to knowledge, mentorship, productive resources and opportunities.

01 Identify and assess beneficiaries.
02 Financial literacy and SME training.
03 Simple business planning.
04 Mentorship and coaching.
05 Productive assets or appropriate financing pathways.
06 Market and partner linkages, monitoring and follow-up.
